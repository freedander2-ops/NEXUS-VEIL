# AUDIO SYSTEM — NEXUS VEIL

## Core Principle

Audio exists to:
support atmosphere,
not dominate it.

The ecosystem should sound:
alive,
minimal,
and reactive.

Avoid:
generic cyberpunk noise,
arcade effects,
or aggressive UI sounds.

---

# 1. Audio Philosophy

Audio should feel:
- tactile
- restrained
- atmospheric
- spatial
- reactive

Interactions should sound:
intentional.

---

# 2. Interaction Audio

## Hover

Soft:
- magnetic ticks
- glass movement
- low digital resonance

Hover sounds must remain:
quiet and subtle.

---

## Click

Clicks should feel:
physical.

Examples:
- muted pulse
- soft bass tap
- restrained synth impact

Avoid:
sharp mechanical clicks.

---

## Transition Audio

Dimension changes may include:
- ambient swells
- signal transitions
- environmental tone shifts

The ecosystem should feel:
continuous.

---

# 3. Environmental Audio

World-state should influence sound behavior.

Examples:

Low Tension:
- calm ambient hum
- slow resonance
- soft environmental drift

High Tension:
- distortion layers
- unstable frequencies
- glitch resonance
- atmospheric pressure

Anomaly States:
- corrupted signal textures
- fragmented ambient pulses
- spatial instability

---

# 4. Audio Structure

Recommended structure:

/public/audio
    /hover
    /click
    /ambient
    /anomaly
    /transitions

Audio files should be:
externally replaceable.

The creator must be able to:
swap sounds
WITHOUT rebuilding the project.

---

# 5. Technical Rules

Avoid:
- autoplay abuse
- large audio payloads
- blocking audio systems
- overlapping chaos

Audio should:
load progressively
and fail gracefully.

---

# 6. Interaction Rules

Not every interaction requires sound.

Silence is important.

The ecosystem should breathe.

---

# 7. Adaptive Audio

Sound behavior may react to:
- time of day
- weather
- tension
- instability
- anomalies
- user interactions

Audio should feel:
environmentally aware.

---

# 8. Accessibility

Provide:
- audio volume controls
- mute system
- reduced audio mode

Never force:
persistent sound playback.

---

# 9. Future Expansion

Possible future systems:
- spatial audio
- reactive ambient layers
- dimensional sound signatures
- anomaly corruption filters

But:
stability and subtlety remain higher priority.

---

# 10. Final Principle

The ecosystem should not:
sound like a game.

It should sound:
like a living digital environment.
