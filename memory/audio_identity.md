# Audio Identity

## Environmental Atmosphere
- Every environment must contain a unique ambient atmosphere (e.g., electronic hums, signal resonance, wind-like data flow).
- Audio deepening immersion without being intrusive.

## Interaction Feedback
- Subtle hover sounds that reflect the "disturbance" of the cursor.
- Physical-sounding clicks or data-burst effects during interaction.
- Atmospheric sound layers that shift during dimensional transitions.

## Anomaly Resonance
- Rare environmental states or secrets should have distinct audio signatures that reward discovery.
