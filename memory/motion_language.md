# Motion Language

## Philosophy
- Motion communicates atmosphere and contains inertia.
- Avoid robotic animations and meaningless particle spam.
- Movement must feel intentional and semi-physical.

## Dimensional Motion
- **Cybersecurity**: High frequency, unstable, aggressive pulses, and threat-space distortions.
- **OSINT**: Low frequency, drifting flow, layered parallax, and spatial expansion.
- **GitHub**: Rhythmic precision, orbital structures, procedural synchronization.

## Inertia & Weight
- Panels and interaction elements should drift slightly with cursor movement and settle with visible weight.
- Transitions are "Dimensional Morphs" that dissolve or shatter the previous state into the next.
