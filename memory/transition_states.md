# Transition States

## Between Sections
- **Cyber to OSINT**: Transition from red/chaos to blue/depth. Calmness increases.
- **OSINT to GitHub**: Transition from blue/layers to white/precision. Sharpness increases.
- **GitHub to Cyber**: Transition from white/stability to red/instability. Tension increases.

## Environmental Triggers
- **Time of Day**: Light levels and color temperature shifts.
- **Weather**: Overlays (rain particles, storm flashes) and mood changes.
- **Pulse Intensity**: Global animation speed and effect frequency (glitch/pulse intensity).
